Swift · Laptop echo check

Manual browser A/B recording. Nothing is uploaded. This tests your laptop browser and selected devices; Pi AEC remains a separate test.

  1. Use speakers, not headphones. Choose your intended microphone/speaker in browser or Windows settings. Keep placement and system/player volume fixed across trials.
  2. Select the same speech WAV/MP3 for each AEC setting. This probe does not control Spotify or ducking; leave volume unchanged and do not change player state during the AEC baseline.
  3. Press Record and follow the prompt: 0–8 s playback with you quiet; 8–16 s speak naturally over the same playback; 16–24 s playback stopped while you continue speaking. The browser records phase timestamps and playback media position.
  4. Repeat with the other AEC setting, then compare both recordings and transcripts. A quieter recording or browser-reported AEC setting does not prove cancellation. The timestamp reference is browser playback state, not sample-aligned acoustic loopback.

Ready when you are

Choose the reference and AEC setting. Microphone starts only when you press Start.

Device settings and recording details

Browser AEC is requested with automatic gain and noise suppression off where supported. Reported settings are configuration evidence, not acoustic proof. Recordings may contain private speech; save locally under the project's var/recordings/ when importing them.
