Notes and recording settings

Use your normal voice. Read each phrase once after the countdown; you can finish early. No sound cue is played. Repeat attempts are preserved rather than overwritten. The level meter is a sampled signal indicator, not a microphone calibration.

Actual browser audio settings will appear after microphone access.

What these recordings can test

Core clips isolate recognition. Other sets preserve corrections, pauses and task requests for controlled replay through ME3’s existing session/tool path. Silence and wake clips are diagnostic inputs. A recording of “stop speaking” alone does not verify actual interruption, speaker leakage, echo cancellation or Raspberry Pi performance. Complex file tasks are captured here; no website, repository or external action is created.
