Save the run's sanitized summary/trace under ignored `var/results/`; do not attach secret-bearing environment/config files.
5. During a later assistant utterance, say a short interruption. Confirm playback stops promptly, the new user transcript appears, and the next answer follows the new turn. Speech interruption must not cancel an unrelated durable task.

Troubleshooting: no page means check bridge startup and VS Code's forwarded-port target; no mic means check the browser permission and selected input; no speech result means inspect sanitized bridge/MCP failure status and ASR request limits; no answer means inspect LLM/provider limits; text without sound means verify selected output and TTS status.
